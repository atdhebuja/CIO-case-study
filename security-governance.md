# Security governance and resilience

## Governance responsibilities

I maintained security policies and a risk register informed by ISO 27001/27002, reviewed risks with the four-person team, and used Asana to follow project and remediation work. I reported to the owner and general manager monthly on risks, progress, expenditure, incidents, and operational indicators.

This describes internal management practices. It is not a claim of ISO certification, a completed independent compliance audit, or a regulated financial-services security mandate.

## Cloud and access oversight

I oversaw cloud and cybersecurity engineering work and established least-privilege access practices across organizational systems. Monthly reporting covered operational health, including availability, patch-remediation progress, and access-control reviews. Numerical improvements are not asserted here.

Least privilege is one security principle; this account does not claim that a complete Zero Trust architecture was implemented.

## Threat intelligence and CERT capability

I led work establishing ICT Academy CERT before my CIO appointment and continued to lead its development. During my CIO period, I integrated and managed MISP for threat-intelligence and indicator sharing. The team's declared constituency includes critical IT businesses, public-sector CIIP, and educational institutions. That constituency description does not imply ownership of national infrastructure or authority as a national CERT.

ICT Academy CERT and UBT-CERT are separate teams. ICT Academy CERT's public TI status is Listed; it is not described here as accredited or FIRST-affiliated.

## Backup and continuity planning

I designed a backup and recovery approach combining cloud-hosted services with local external storage intended to remain offline between backup operations. This provided a secondary recovery copy outside the cloud environment.

The public account omits live system details, storage locations, client information, and recovery credentials. No independently verified recovery time, recovery point, complete coverage, or guaranteed protection against ransomware is claimed. A backup design is distinct from demonstrated recovery capability; restoration tests would be needed to substantiate recovery performance.

## Internal assurance and vendors

I reviewed business processes against documented operating and security expectations, monitored vendor deliverables and service commitments, and scoped customer requirements into technical specifications and statements of work. These were management reviews and contract oversight, not independent audit opinions.
