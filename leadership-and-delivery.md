# Leadership and delivery

## Organizational scope

ICT Academy had four employees including me during the period described. I reported to the owner and general manager in regular monthly meetings and oversaw technical work across security, cloud, and networking functions. I managed and reported IT/security spending; no budget amount or financial return is published in this account.

## Customer and partner responsibilities

I represented ICT Academy in customer meetings and professional events, maintained partner relationships, scoped customer requirements, prepared technical specifications and statements of work, and drafted and negotiated service contracts. I reviewed vendor deliverables and service-level commitments.

## Training capability

I led the process for ICT Academy to become an EC-Council Authorized Training Center. The organization's [March 2024 announcement](https://www.academyict.net/ict-academy-becomes-ec-council-accredited-training-center/) records the institutional milestone. This organizational status is separate from my individual Certified CISO, CEH, and instructor credentials.

## Municipal capacity building

I led delivery of a workshop for municipal IT officers on information technology and cybersecurity in municipal services. ICT Academy's [May 2024 report](https://www.academyict.net/advancing-information-technology-and-cybersecurity-in-kosovos-municipal-services/) describes collaboration with the Association of Kosovo Municipalities and names me among the presenters. This account does not claim an attendance count or measured improvement across every municipality.

## Research project leadership

As R&D Team Lead, a concurrent appointment from September 2020 to July 2024, I led a six-person research project with Global Cyber Alliance. The work used GCA AIDE data and Python-based predictive models for IoT attack detection. I coordinated dataset analysis, model design and training, and publication activity.

The project-team size is distinct from ICT Academy's four-person employee count. This account does not describe the research prototypes as a production security service, a revenue-generating product, or an externally audited deployment.

## Leadership reflection

This role required connecting technical work with organizational choices: defining how services were delivered, reviewing risks and expenditure, setting expectations for partners, and making research outputs understandable to customers. The clearest evidence is the work product and the decision it supported, rather than the seniority of the job title alone.
