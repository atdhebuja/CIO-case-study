# Operating model and business processes

## Problem

Business processes were not consistently defined, and the transition from research work into consulting services needed a clearer structure. My objective was to define how work moved through the organization and make responsibilities, inputs, outputs, and review points easier to manage.

## My contribution

I reorganized the operating structure around four functional areas and documented their processes using Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN). These were functional areas within a four-person company, not four separately staffed departments.

| Area | Process coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Education | Course design, training delivery, and completion/certification administration |
| Research and Development | Idea development, research execution, results, and publication |
| Consulting | Client engagement, solution design, implementation, support, evaluation, and feedback |
| Activities | Event planning, outreach, delivery, and follow-up |
| Research to Consulting | Defined the connection between research outputs and potential consulting services |

Certification administration in this table does not mean ICT Academy independently awarded third-party professional credentials.

## Implementation

I established a process repository using Red Hat Process Automation Manager to organize BPMN models and related documentation, and standardized an R&D register. I used ISO 9001 principles and Plan–Do–Check–Act review to organize process documentation, KPIs, and feedback.

BPMN was the modeling notation. Business process analysis supported examination of workflows; documenting a process does not by itself establish that it was automated. This case study does not claim every modeled workflow was deployed as an executable process.

## Deliverables and limits

The work produced a defined operating structure, documented process areas, a shared repository, and an R&D register. These provided a basis for assigning work and reviewing delivery. Quantified efficiency, cost, and commercialization outcomes are not available in this public account.
