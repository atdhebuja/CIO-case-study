# CIO leadership reflection

**Atdhe Buja | ICT Academy | January 2022–July 2024**

This is a retrospective reflection based on my account of the role, not a contemporaneous management record.

In a four-person organization, my CIO responsibilities combined governance with close involvement in technical delivery. I connected technology priorities with education, research, consulting, and professional activities, while reporting to the owner and general manager.

A central challenge was making the transition from research outputs to potential consulting services more explicit. My work on process definitions, a BPMN repository, and the research register gave that transition a documented structure. Those deliverables do not, by themselves, demonstrate commercial success or automation.

For security and continuity, I combined policies, risk reviews, remediation follow-up, least-privilege practices, and a secondary backup approach. The distinction I emphasize in this portfolio is between designing a control, operating it, and verifying its effectiveness.

For a future implementation, I would define baseline measures and acceptance criteria at the outset: who owns the process, what evidence shows it works, when it is reviewed, and who decides on unresolved risks. This is a proposed improvement approach, not a claim about an undocumented historical result.
